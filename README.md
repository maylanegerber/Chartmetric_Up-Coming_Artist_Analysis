## Original Capstone & Idea
The music industry, is a testament to the constant evolution of art, technology, and commerce. From its earliest origins as a live performance-centered industry, it has continually adapted and transformed to reflect the changing tastes, technologies, and societal norms of its time. Whether it was the introduction of recorded music, the rise of digital downloads, the streaming revolution, or the current era of data-driven decision-making, the music industry is marked by perpetual change. This unceasing transformation not only reflects the innovation and adaptability of musicians and industry professionals, but also the profound influence of cultural shifts and technological breakthroughs on the way we create, consume, and appreciate music. In this Capstone, we will delve into the ever-shifting landscape of the music industry and how it has redefined itself in response to social media and Big Data.

The emergence of using data to sign artists to labels represents a significant shift in the music industry landscape. With the advent of advanced analytics and streaming platforms, record labels and talent scouts are increasingly turning to data-driven insights to identify promising artists and predict their potential success. These data-driven approaches offer the promise of making the industry more efficient and reducing the risks associated with traditional talent scouting. However, this trend also raises a host of complex issues. First, it can commodify music, reducing the art form to mere statistics and trends, potentially stifling creativity, and diversity. Second, it may favor artists who are skilled at gaming algorithms over those with genuine talent, homogenizing the musical landscape. Additionally, reliance on data alone risks overlooking the intangible qualities that make artists truly exceptional, such as authenticity and emotional depth. Balancing data with human judgment remains a challenge in an industry grappling with the implications of this digital revolution.

One such study was done by The Pudding looking at the artists who made it onto Spotify's charts between January 2020 and December 2021. Of those there were 332 who were newcomers to the charts and of that number a quarter of them were newly charting artists who gained their initial popularity through TikTok (Daniels). Their study then went on to investigate the actual impact of going viral on the career of these artists by looking at 8 factors: Spotify playlisted new songs, Played Music Festival, Played Live Show, 100K+ Instagram Followers, 100K+ YouTube Subscribers, 1M+ Spotify Monthly Listeners, Charted with new songs, and Signed Record Deal. They used data to retrospectively find which artists had been able to make it big due to social media and how to measure that success. 

I am passionate about both music and data and have been looking for ways to merge these two interests within my career. I wanted to create a project where not only I could learn about the current operating model of the industry, but also where I could see how I could improve it. I am seeking to look towards data to find artists before they become mainstream while weighing different factors holistically and with the knowledge that social media virality is not a sustainable or accurate representation of long-term success. The jobs of A&Rs around the world have changed considerably due to the increase of data availability and data driven decision making but this should not eclipse the true artistry of the musicians. My process will attempt to take into account these new social media platforms that allow for artists to have agency in their careers while weeding out those whose fame will die out after a dance trend has run its course.



## My Process
## Discovering Undiscovered Artists
In pursuit of identifying and showcasing emerging artists who have yet to gain widespread recognition, I employed Chartmetric's A&R dataset—a valuable resource that curates a list of promising talents exhibiting notable metrics-driven growth. This list is meticulously sorted based on the increase in relevant metrics, prominently featuring a 30-day growth rate and being regularly updated on a weekly basis. This criteria effectively singles out artists experiencing a substantial percentage surge in their fan base, enabling me to collect comprehensive data from both Spotify and TikTok platforms over a span of four weeks, commencing from Sept 15 to October 6th. It's worth noting that these A&R data compilations undergo weekly refreshes to encompass the latest trending artists on the selected platforms.

In the early stages of my research, a pivotal decision involved assessing an artist's potential for sustained social media success rather than a brief meteoric rise. My methodology aimed at ensuring a prolonged engagement with their fan base. I adopted a dual-platform approach by incorporating data from both Spotify and TikTok, a choice driven by diversification and the knowledge that both attract different interaction from their respective audiences. Over the course of four weeks, I meticulously extracted CSV files containing A&R data for approximately 1000 artists per platform, rigorously refining the dataset each week. This iterative process involved the exclusion of artists affiliated with major record labels, thereby excluding those who had already achieved substantial recognition. Moreover, I focused exclusively on artists within the United States and meticulously cleaned the data to ensure uniformity in data types.

Each week, I performed this data filtering process, which ultimately resulted in a refined dataset of artists who successfully met the criteria of remaining relevant in the U.S. for a minimum of four weeks, unaided by major record labels.

## Finding a Fanbase
With this curated shortlist in hand, I embarked on a detailed analysis of each artist listed in Chartmetric. This comprehensive assessment led to the creation of an Excel spreadsheet for each artist, encompassing an extensive array of 75 variables. These variables included, but were not limited to, genre classification, record label affiliation, past and upcoming concert engagements, festival appearances, solo tour ventures, streaming achievements (e.g., songs with over 1 million streams and total songs released), weekly and monthly fluctuations in Spotify followers, monthly listeners on Spotify, Instagram followers, TikTok followers, weekly and monthly changes in TikTok followers, weekly changes and monthly fluctuations in TikTok engagement rates, and several others.

In order to further refine the list, I introduced additional criteria and created custom columns such as "Above_300K_Spotify_Monthly_Followers," "Above_100K_TikTok_Monthly_Followers," and "TikTok_Engagement_Above_5%." Additionally, I manually gathered information on the number of live concerts that each artist had scheduled or previously performed—a key consideration in assessing an artist's potential.

It's pertinent to underscore that contemporary A&R practices often prioritize artists based on viral moments, yet I hold the belief that this approach lacks analytical rigor. The prevalence of viral content in today's landscape has reduced the significance of such moments. Merely signing an artist based on the serendipitous success of a single video within an algorithm is a suboptimal strategy. In my view, more meaningful metrics revolve around fanbase cultivation and sustainability.

With my initial pool of 136 artists, I commenced the process of fine-tuning my selection, leveraging these meticulously curated criteria to identify those with the potential for sustained artistic careers. Below are a couple graphs depicting the artists that were filtered for up to this point.



## What to Measure?
The initial criterion I employed for filtering artists was their Spotify followers. I opted to prioritize Spotify over TikTok for several reasons. TikTok, while a platform that garners attention, doesn't necessarily reflect a deep connection with the artistry itself. Merely having viewers engage with videos on TikTok doesn't equate to genuine dedication to an artist's work. Moreover, I chose to focus on followers rather than listeners because I sought to identify true fans—individuals who consciously decide to engage with an artist's music. I aimed to exclude those who passively encounter an artist on a Spotify playlist in the background. Consequently, I set the threshold at artists with over 75,000 Spotify followers.

The next parameter I considered was an artist's live performance history or scheduled live shows. Listening to an artist online doesn't always translate to a willingness to attend a live performance, particularly one for which a fan is willing to pay. Selecting artists with a live performance track record validated the existence of a genuine fanbase. It also signified that these artists possessed the ability to deliver live performances beyond the constraints of a 30-second video, devoid of editing or retakes. After subjecting my initial list of 136 artists to these two filters, I was left with a shortlist of 33 artists. However, I found myself at a crossroads. While I contemplated applying further filters based on additional metrics, I realized that I needed a more holistic understanding of these artists. It was imperative to shift from treating them as mere data points and embark on a journey to truly know them.

The Artists Behind the Datapoints
This journey led me to delve into each artist individually, examining their backgrounds, the duration of their musical pursuits, the tracks that had garnered attention and the reasons behind their appeal, their engagement levels on social media, the nature of their content on various social media platforms, their collaborations, and ultimately, their identities as artists. I listened to their music extensively and began to grasp the essence of each artist's persona; I was beginning to connect with them on a personal level. However, I also encountered a challenge in this process—I couldn't help but be influenced by my own music preferences. As a fan of indie alternative pop music, my affinity was naturally inclined toward artists within that genre, making it challenging for me to fully appreciate promising rap artists, for example. While I gravitated towards artists like Emei, Mackenzy Macay, and KiNG MALA, I felt less connected to artists such as Calboy and Dowba Montana. To overcome this potential bias and make more objective decisions, I returned to the data-driven approach, this time with a graphical perspective.

## My Top 5
After getting to know these artists and looking at the data again I filtered one more time by only allowing myself to pick an artist that wasn’t already signed to an indie label backed or somehow affiliated by a major. This eliminated quite a few artists including Sexxy Red, That Mexican OT, Autumn!, Sam Barber, and Samara Joy. With all the knowledge that I had gathered up to this point and careful analysis of how these artists compared to one another my Top 5 materialized itself: Mackenzy Mackay, Emei, KiNG MALA, Sofia Isella, and Obongjayer. They each had a unique blend of artistry and fanbase that made me believe in their career future. 

Mackenzy Mackay, while following the familiar path of a typical TikTok artist, stands out for his remarkable ability to transform casual viewers into devoted fans. He has experienced consistent growth across all platforms over the past year, particularly on Spotify, where his streams continue to rise with each new release. Notably, one of his tracks, released in May 2023, has already garnered an impressive 9 million streams, while two other notable songs dropped in July and October of the same year. Mackenzy's performances reveal a genuine commitment to his craft. However, there remains a question of whether he can break free from the social media persona that has come to define him, as his content mostly consists of typical bedroom performances – a format now expected by his growing fanbase.

 Emei, on the other hand, quickly became one of my personal favorites due to her exceptional musical talent and captivating sound. With just 18 songs in her repertoire, she has achieved remarkable success, with 13 of her tracks surpassing 1 million streams. Although her popularity peaked at the beginning of 2023 with hits like "Scatterbrain" and "Irresponsible," her recent streaming figures have experienced a decline, though her latest collaboration with NOTD, released on November 10th, reached 665K streams in under two weeks – possibly attributed to NOTD's fanbase. Emei's unique aesthetic and clear potential for mainstream success position her as a future celebrity in today's pop culture scene. Her upcoming tours across Europe and the US, featuring substantial venues like the El Rey in Los Angeles, solidify her status as an artist on the rise.

KiNG MALA earns a place in my top 5 with her distinctive alternative pop sound. While she has witnessed growth across various platforms, her recent momentum has somewhat slowed down. Despite this, her biggest hit boasts an impressive 25 million streams. KiNG MALA's fanbase remains engaged, as evidenced by her ongoing tour with several upcoming dates in the next few weeks. Notably, when compared to other top artists, she excels in terms of the ratio of viral songs relative to her followership, indicating her ability to convert listeners into dedicated fans across multiple platforms.

Sofia Isella's musical journey is nothing short of fascinating. Initially trained as a classical violinist, she daringly transitioned into the realm of alternative indie rock. Despite her early releases in 2020 receiving limited attention, Sofia's 2022 track, "Independent," became a sensation, amassing nearly 1 million listens. Subsequently, all her releases have enjoyed widespread acclaim, with almost all surpassing 1 million streams. This surge in listenership was accompanied by a significant increase in followers across all platforms, affording her the opportunity to embark on a tour that kicked off in Australia and the UK, with several upcoming shows scheduled in the US. Furthermore, the themes of her songs, addressing anti-patriarchal and pressing societal issues, resonate deeply with today's cultural landscape. Sofia Isella's artistic journey represents an inspiring example of evolution and dedication in the music industry.

Lastly, I chose Obongjayer. He is a bit of an outlier and gamble because he has incredible artistry and has garnered many streams through collaborations, but these have not translated to huge fanbases on these social media platforms: on Instagram he has 116K, on TikTok 15K, and on Spotify with 82K. He has incredible artistry in his unique sound and the way he portrays himself through his album art and on platform, but he has yet to really gain traction of a fanbase. While he is struggling to gain fans in the likes of the average listener, it feels like the other growing stars see him and want him to succeed. He has collaborated with the likes of Fred Again (on “Adore You” with 60 million streams), Little Simz (on “Point and Kill” with 26 million streams) and Jeshi (on “Protein” with 7 million streams). In fact, his top 7 most streamed songs are all collaborations with other artists. Although these partnerships might seem to overshadow his individual achievements, they instead speak to Obongjayer's significant platform and burgeoning reach. His artistic integrity, coupled with his ability to collaborate successfully, suggests a latent potential for growth.

## Final Decision
Sofia Isella stands out as an exceptional choice for recognition and support in the music industry for several compelling reasons. Her journey as an artist is marked by a remarkable transformation from a classically trained violinist to an emerging indie rock sensation. This transition showcases her versatility as an artist, as she fearlessly ventured into a new musical genre to express herself.

One of the most notable aspects of Sofia's career is her steady and impressive rise to prominence. In 2020, her initial releases may not have garnered significant attention, but this did not deter her from pursuing her passion. Remarkably, within just two years, Sofia achieved a monumental breakthrough with her 2022 song, "Independent," which amassed nearly 1 million listens. This remarkable success serves as a testament to her talent, determination, and unwavering commitment to her craft.

What truly sets Sofia Isella apart is her consistency in captivating audiences. Subsequent releases have continued to resonate with a broad and enthusiastic audience, each accumulating over 1 million streams. This extraordinary achievement reflects her ability to craft music that resonates deeply with listeners, transcending boundaries and genres to create a unique and memorable sound.

Sofia's impressive musical journey has not been limited to online streaming platforms. Her meteoric rise in listenership was paralleled by a substantial increase in her social media following. She has over 400K followers on both TikTok and Instagram and multiple of her recent videos have gone viral with millions of streams. In today’s industry it is important to have both a presence on and offline. This surge in followers across various platforms underscores her strong and engaged fan base, further solidifying her position as a rising star in the music industry. 

Sofia's decision to embark on a tour, commencing in Australia and the UK and expanding to include upcoming dates in the US, is a testament to her growing international appeal. Her ability to connect with audiences from diverse cultural backgrounds demonstrates her global potential as an artist.

Perhaps equally important as her musical achievements are the themes she explores in her songs. Sofia Isella uses her platform to address vital issues in today's society, particularly focusing on anti-patriarchal themes. Her willingness to tackle important societal topics through her music reflects a strong sense of social responsibility and a commitment to using her art as a means of advocating for change.

In summary, Sofia Isella emerges as a top choice for recognition and support due to her exceptional musical talent, resilience, consistent success, engaged fan base, and her commitment to addressing important societal issues through her art. Sofia Isella is undoubtedly a promising artist poised for continued growth and impact in the music industry.

Remarks on Limitations & Study Framework
The intersection of data analytics and talent scouting in the music industry is a double-edged sword that requires careful navigation. While data offers valuable insights and the promise of trend prediction, it is crucial to recognize the limitations of a quantitative approach. The inherent bias of the listener, the researcher, or the A&R executive in interpreting data can skew the results, potentially sidelining unique talents that do not conform to current trends or algorithmic visibility. Even trying to be as unbiased as possible my methodology was not perfect, and it is very possible that with the same exact data someone who had other genre tendencies or priorities would have selected another artist as the one they see the most potential in. If I were to redo this study, I would select a longer time frame and attempt to find a methodology that was less biased in my holistic approach to talent. The ethical issues with how talent is discovered today also speaks to the social responsibility new entrants in the industry have to preserve talent and the creation of music by artists. I tried to factor true artistry into my approach and ranking of artist but this is a very difficult variable to measure and quantify. 

## Conclusion
Now that platforms such as TikTok have matured it is important to recognize the transient impact of social media virality, emphasizing the need for a balanced approach that values artistic integrity and the intrinsic qualities that define musical talent beyond a 30 second edited. As we move forward, the industry must strive to maintain a harmony between data-driven methodologies and the subjective, nuanced art of talent recognition. Embracing the power of data while acknowledging its limitations will be key to nurturing a diverse, dynamic, and sustainable musical ecosystem where artists get more agency in their musical careers and A&R departments can be a made aware of talent that otherwise would have never crossed their path.
